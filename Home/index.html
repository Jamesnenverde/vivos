THE UNIVERSITY OF MANILA

Founded 1913

UM HISTORY

On October 5, 1913, a group of four young men and a woman, with ages from fifteen to twenty-one, met for breakfast at the Palma de Mallorca, a popular restaurant beside the Sto. Domingo Church in Intramuros, Manila. The five were Dr. Apolinario G. de los Santos, his brother Dr. Mariano V. de los Santos, his sister Maria de los Santos, and two friends Dr. Buenaventura J. Bello and Atty. Antonio Rivero.

These impecunious young people encountered many challenges in realizing their dream of establishing an institution whose motto was "Patria, Scientia, et Virtus." They named their school Instituto de Manila, and Apolinario de los Santos was elected as the first director.

KEY MILES STONE

1913

Instituto de Manila founded by five young visionaries in Intramuros.

1919

College of Law formally opened; graduates top the Bar Examinations.

1921

Re-incorporated as the University of Manila on November 3 under Act No. 1459.

1938

Emmanuel Pelaez, future Vice-President of the Philippines, tops the Bar Examinations.

1939

Nazi Germany invaded Poland, marking the beginning of World War II.

1941

Philippines joined World War II after the surprise attack on Pearl Harbor.

1942

Empire of Japan took control of Manila; The University of Manila closed during occupation.

1945

The University of Manila reopened after World War II, following the liberation of the Philippines by American forces.

1959

Madame Helen de los Santos takes the presidency following Dr. Mariano's passing.

1981

Dr. Virgilio de los Santos assumes presidency; receives Japan's Rising Sun Medal of Honor.

1994

Four-year courses in Hotel & Restaurant Management and Computer Science launched.

2003

First university in the Philippines to offer a degree in the Japanese Language.

2006

Dr. Emily Dodson De Leon elected President; becomes PACU's first Vice-President in 2007.

2019

Total Lockdown in the Philippines; University of Manila adapts to the new normal of classes.

2026

Atty. Dave De los Santos elected President.
